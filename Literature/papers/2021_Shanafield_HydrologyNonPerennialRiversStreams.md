# One-Sentence Takeaway | 一句话总结

> Non-perennial flow is an emergent outcome of interacting climate, groundwater, catchment, channel, and human controls—not a state determined by discharge alone—and surface-water presence must not be treated automatically as evidence of connected flow. 非永久性水流是气候、地下水、流域、河道和人类活动共同作用的结果，不能由流量单独决定；遥感观测到地表水也不能自动等同于存在连通水流。

# What This Paper Tells Me | 这篇论文告诉了我什么

## 1. Core Idea / Research Question | 核心问题

This review builds a global hydrological understanding of non-perennial rivers and streams. It synthesizes how streamflow is generated, how water is lost from channels, why flowing networks expand and contract, how climate, geology, vegetation, and human activity control intermittence, and why standard river-monitoring and modeling tools often perform poorly in these systems. It concludes with ten research directions for improving prediction and management (pp. 1, 3, 12–15).

这篇综述试图建立一个全球适用的非永久性河流水文学框架：水流如何产生，河道如何失水，有水河网为何扩张与收缩，气候、地质、植被和人类活动如何共同控制间歇性，以及传统河流观测和模型为什么在这类系统中容易失效。最后，作者提出10项未来研究方向（第1、3、12–15页）。

## 2. Key Concepts and Definitions | 核心概念与定义

- **Non-perennial river / stream | 非永久性河流 / 溪流:** A flowing watercourse that does not maintain continuous surface-water flow throughout the year (p. 1). The paper notes that there is no widely used rule specifying how many no-flow days or how much non-flowing reach length is required for classification (p. 1). 指一年中不能持续维持地表水流的流水水体；目前没有被广泛接受的停流天数或停流河段长度标准（第1页）。
- **Intermittent | 间歇性:** Commonly used for seasonal flow (p. 2). 通常指具有季节性流动的河流（第2页）。
- **Ephemeral | 短暂性 / 暴雨响应型:** Commonly used when flow is short-lived and occurs directly in response to precipitation (p. 2). 通常指水流持续时间短且直接响应降水事件的河流（第2页）。
- **Flow regime | 流态:** The magnitude, frequency, duration, and timing of streamflow, together with how rapidly these properties change through time (p. 3). 流量大小、频率、持续时间、发生时机及其时间变化速率的组合（第3页）。
- **Baseflow | 基流:** Groundwater contribution to a river (p. 4). 地下水对河流的补给（第4页）。
- **Transmission loss | 传输损失:** Stream water lost along a channel, driven largely by the hydraulic gradient between the streambed and regional water table and controlled by streambed and geological properties. It includes groundwater recharge, unsaturated-zone storage, and evapotranspiration rather than recharge alone (p. 4). 河水沿河道减少的过程，主要由河床与区域地下水位之间的水力梯度驱动，并受河床和地质性质控制；它包括地下水补给、非饱和带储水及蒸散，而不能简单等同于地下水补给（第4页）。
- **Flow permanence versus surface-water persistence | 水流持续性与地表水持续存在:** A reach can lack surface flow while still containing isolated pools, springs, moist sediment, or subsurface connections (p. 6). 河段即使没有地表流，也可能仍有孤立水池、泉、湿润沉积物或地下水连接（第6页）。

## 3. Main Insights / Findings | 核心内容与主要发现

### 3.1 Non-perennial rivers are not restricted to drylands | 非永久性河流并非干旱区特有

Approximately half of the world's flowing waterways are described as non-perennial, but they occur across alpine headwaters, lowlands, seasonally frozen and permafrost regions, karst terrain, and tropical, temperate, continental, dry, and polar climates (pp. 1–2, 6–8). Drylands contain many such rivers, yet high evapotranspiration, seasonal freezing, subsurface karst drainage, groundwater fluctuations, and human water use can also produce intermittence outside arid environments.

论文指出全球约一半流水水体属于非永久性，但其分布覆盖高山源头、低地、季节冻土、多年冻土、喀斯特以及热带、温带、大陆性、干旱和极地气候（第1–2、6–8页）。除干旱外，高蒸散、季节冻结、喀斯特地下排水、地下水位波动及人类用水也能造成间歇性。

**Page:** 1–2, 6–8

### 3.2 Flow activation is a threshold-like but multivariable process | 水流启动具有阈值特征，但由多变量共同控制

Precipitation or snowmelt initiates most non-perennial flow, but whether channel flow actually develops depends on soil and geology, antecedent soil moisture, groundwater depth, rainfall intensity and duration, topography, and preferential pathways (pp. 3–4). In catchments with thick unsaturated zones, saturation-excess flow is unlikely to be widespread and infiltration-excess overland flow may dominate. In wet or thawing systems, saturation-excess flow and groundwater contributions can be important.

降雨或融雪是多数非永久性河流启动的外部驱动力，但是否形成河道水流，还取决于土壤与地质、前期土壤水分、地下水埋深、降雨强度和历时、地形及优先流路径（第3–4页）。厚非饱和带流域中，蓄满产流通常不易大范围发生，超渗产流可能占主导；较湿或季节解冻系统则可能受到蓄满产流和地下水补给控制。

**Page:** 3–4

### 3.3 Groundwater connection strongly controls persistence | 地下水联系强烈控制水流持续性

Groundwater outflow may occur where the water table intersects topographic lows or aquifer boundaries, either as discrete springs or over kilometres of channel. For a reach to cease flowing, this groundwater contribution must cease during at least part of the year in at least some reaches (p. 4). Mediterranean streams, for example, may continue into the dry season only where groundwater baseflow persists (p. 7).

地下水可在地下水位与地形低点或含水层边界相交处出流，表现为泉或延伸数千米的河道补给。一个河段要停止流动，其地下水补给必须至少在一年中的某段时间、某些河段中停止（第4页）。例如，地中海气候区的溪流只有在存在地下水基流时才可能延续到旱季（第7页）。

**Page:** 4, 7

### 3.4 Transmission losses can reduce downstream discharge and channel size | 传输损失会降低下游流量并改变河道尺度

Most non-perennial rivers are losing rivers for much or all of the year. Loss rates depend on the hydraulic gradient, streambed hydraulic conductivity and moisture, and underlying geology. Longitudinal losses can decrease channel width and capacity over relatively short distances (p. 4). In drylands, channel infiltration is often a major recharge pathway, but part of the apparent loss may remain in the unsaturated zone or be consumed by vegetation and evaporation (pp. 4–5).

多数非永久性河流在一年大部分时间或全年表现为失水河流。失水速率取决于水力梯度、河床水力传导率与含水状态以及下伏地质；沿程损失可在较短距离内造成河宽与河道容量下降（第4页）。在干旱区，河道入渗往往是重要补给途径，但部分表观损失可能停留在非饱和带，或被植被和蒸发消耗（第4–5页）。

**Page:** 4–5

### 3.5 A river network can wet and dry in several spatial directions | 河网可沿不同空间方向湿润或干涸

Flowing networks do not simply retract upstream from a fixed perennial channel. Seasonal headwater flow may expand upstream as groundwater rises; storm flow may start in mountain headwaters and propagate downstream; or isolated pools may connect as the wetted network expands. Multiple modes may coexist within one catchment because geology, topography, and groundwater vary spatially (p. 5; Figure 2).

有水河网并不只是从固定的永久性河段向上游简单收缩。季节性源头河流可能随地下水位上升向上游扩张；暴雨水流可能从山地源头向下游推进；孤立水池也可能在湿润范围扩张时相互连通。同一流域内可因地质、地形和地下水空间差异同时存在多种模式（第5页；图2）。

**Page:** 5

### 3.6 Surface-water detection is not the same as detecting flow | 遥感识别地表水不等于识别水流

Once surface flow ceases, pools or springs can persist within or beside the channel for most or all of the year and may remain connected by subsurface flow. Moist streambeds and isolated pools are ecologically important refuges (p. 6). A water-presence product can therefore label a no-flow reach as wet, while a flow-permanence product should distinguish connected flow, ponding, moist bed, and dry bed.

地表流停止后，河道内部或邻近位置的水池和泉仍可能在一年大部分时间甚至全年存在，并可能通过地下水相连；湿润河床和孤立水池还是重要生态庇护所（第6页）。因此，水体存在产品可能把无流河段标记为“有水”，而水流持续性产品应区分连通水流、积水、湿润河床和干河床。

**Page:** 6

### 3.7 No observation method captures all relevant scales | 没有一种观测方法能够覆盖全部相关尺度

Manual surveys provide detailed channel information but weak spatial and temporal coverage. Satellite data expand spatial coverage but often have pixels too coarse for small streams and offer sparse snapshots; low-altitude LiDAR and UAV imagery provide finer detail but face canopy, regulatory, sensor, and processing constraints. Citizen science broadens coverage but depends on participation and training. Temperature, resistance, float, flow, and water-level sensors improve temporal monitoring, yet high-energy floods, scour, deposition, and sediment mobility can destroy equipment or invalidate measurements (pp. 9–10).

人工调查能提供精细河道信息，却难以获得广泛时空覆盖；卫星遥感扩大空间范围，但像元常过粗且只是稀疏时间快照；低空LiDAR和无人机影像分辨率更高，却受树冠、法规、传感器和数据处理限制。公民科学可扩大覆盖，但依赖持续参与和培训。温度、电阻、浮子、流动和水位传感器能够改善时间监测，却可能被高能洪水、冲刷、淤积和泥沙运动破坏或造成测量失效（第9–10页）。

**Page:** 9–10

### 3.8 Standard discharge and infiltration methods contain systematic biases | 常规流量与入渗方法存在系统偏差

Most non-perennial rivers are ungauged or have only one main-stem gauge. Scour and deposition make rating curves unstable, so differences between upstream and downstream gauges can contain large discharge errors (p. 11). Point infiltration tests during dry conditions assume that shallow streambed properties remain valid during real floods, yet clogging, preferential pathways, changing groundwater levels, and persistent unsaturation can make actual event infiltration either higher or lower (pp. 10–11).

多数非永久性河流没有流量站，或仅在干流设置一个站点。冲刷和淤积使水位—流量关系不稳定，因此上下游站点差值可能包含很大的流量误差（第11页）。无流期点尺度入渗试验假定浅层河床性质可代表真实洪水，但表面堵塞、优先流、地下水位变化和持续非饱和状态都可能使事件期实际入渗高于或低于试验结果（第10–11页）。

**Page:** 10–11

### 3.9 Models must represent dry–wet transitions and subsurface processes | 模型必须表达干—湿转换和地下过程

Rainfall–runoff models face uncertainty in groundwater contributions, antecedent conditions, and subsurface flow paths. Flashy hydrographs and long periods of zero flow give limited calibration information, especially for ephemeral streams. Artificial neural networks have sometimes matched or exceeded conventional rainfall–runoff models, but physically based integrated surface–subsurface models remain rare because parameters are scarce, catchments are heterogeneous, and abrupt dry-to-wet transitions are numerically difficult (pp. 11–12).

降雨—径流模型面临地下水贡献、前期条件和地下流路的不确定性；陡涨陡落过程及长期零流量使率定信息不足，短暂性河流尤其如此。人工神经网络在一些案例中达到或超过传统模型表现，但物理基础的地表—地下水耦合模型仍很少使用，因为参数资料缺乏、流域异质性强，且突然干—湿转换具有数值困难（第11–12页）。

**Page:** 11–12

### 3.10 The major gaps are process understanding and co-located data | 核心缺口是过程认识和共址数据

The ten research priorities include flow-generation and cessation mechanisms; co-located hydrologic, ecological, geomorphic, and biogeochemical data; flow–water-quality links; hyporheic processes; sediment effects; persistent pools; geological controls; recharge; plant–water interactions; and better global recognition of small and arid-region rivers (pp. 12–15). These priorities emphasize that classification should be linked to mechanisms rather than treated as a purely cartographic exercise.

作者提出的10项重点包括产流与停流机制，共址的水文—生态—地貌—生物地球化学数据，水流—水质联系，潜流带过程，泥沙效应，永久水池，地质控制，地下水补给，植物—水相互作用，以及更准确的全球小河流和干旱区河流制图（第12–15页）。这些重点说明，分类必须连接物理机制，而不能只作为制图问题处理。

**Page:** 12–15

## 4. Mechanisms / Relationships | 机制与变量关系

### Flow generation | 产流

```text
Rainfall / snowmelt
        ↓
rainfall intensity + duration
+ antecedent soil moisture
+ soil / geology / preferential pathways
+ depth to groundwater
        ↓
overland flow + interflow + groundwater outflow
        ↓
channel flow activation and flow duration
```

降水并不会自动转化为河道流量。只有在降雨强度和历时、前期湿度、土壤/地质、优先流路径及地下水埋深共同满足条件时，地表径流、壤中流或地下水出流才会形成河道水流（第3–4页）。

### Flow persistence and cessation | 水流持续与停止

```text
Water inputs
(runoff + interflow + baseflow + upstream inflow)
        versus
Water losses
(streambed infiltration + unsaturated storage + ET)
        ↓
positive reach water balance → flow persists
negative reach water balance → discharge declines / flow ceases
```

对单个河段而言，持续性本质上取决于输入和损失的竞争。即使上游有流量，强河床入渗、非饱和带储水和蒸散仍可能使下游停流（第4–5页）。

### Geometry and permanence | 河道几何与持续性

```text
Discharge + channel geometry
        are modified by
transmission loss + sediment scour/deposition
        and interact with
slope + groundwater connectivity + streambed properties
        ↓
water depth, velocity, connectivity, and flow permanence
```

河宽或横截面积不是静态外部控制量：沿程失水可缩小河宽和容量，泥沙冲刷/淤积又会改变河床几何与入渗条件（第4、10、13页）。因此，`Q_threshold = f(A)` 可能捕捉部分水力尺度效应，但 `A` 本身也可能是水文和地貌过程的结果。

### Climate, geology, vegetation, and people | 气候、地质、植被与人类活动

```text
Climate → precipitation, snow/ice melt, ET demand
Geology → slope, permeability, storage, groundwater exchange
Vegetation → catchment retention and riparian ET
Human activity → abstraction, dams, groundwater drawdown, climate change
                         ↓
               flow-regime intermittence
```

四类控制并非独立作用，而是共同塑造流态（第6–9页）。

## 5. Important Distinctions / Caveats | 重要区分与注意事项

- **Surface water ≠ flowing water. | 地表水存在 ≠ 河流正在流动。** Persistent pools and springs can remain after connected flow stops (p. 6). 连通流停止后仍可能存在永久水池或泉（第6页）。
- **Transmission loss ≠ groundwater recharge. | 传输损失 ≠ 地下水补给。** Transmission loss also includes unsaturated storage and evapotranspiration (p. 4). 它还包括非饱和带储存和蒸散（第4页）。
- **Non-perennial ≠ arid river. | 非永久性河流 ≠ 干旱区河流。** Non-perennial systems occur in all major climates (pp. 6–8). 所有主要气候类型中均可出现非永久性河流（第6–8页）。
- **A dry reach ≠ a dry river everywhere. | 某河段干涸 ≠ 整条河流处处干涸。** Wetting and drying fronts move through networks in different directions (p. 5). 湿润与干涸前缘可沿不同方向传播（第5页）。
- **Flow regime ≠ discharge magnitude alone. | 流态 ≠ 单一流量大小。** Frequency, duration, timing, and change rate also matter (p. 3). 频率、历时、时机和变化速率同样重要（第3页）。
- **No universal cessation threshold is established. | 尚无通用停流阈值。** The review reports no accepted number of dry days, dry-reach length, discharge, or geometry threshold (p. 1). 本文没有给出公认的干涸天数、干涸河段长度、流量或几何阈值（第1页）。
- **Gauge absence and zero-flow records both require scrutiny. | 无站资料和零流量记录都需谨慎解释。** Gauges preferentially sample perennial rivers, while scour and rating-curve instability affect non-perennial discharge estimates (pp. 2, 11). 站网偏向永久性河流，冲刷和率定曲线不稳定又会影响非永久性流量记录（第2、11页）。

# Methods and Evidence | 方法与证据

## Methodology | 方法（Optional）

### Overall approach

- This is a narrative, conceptual review that integrates primary studies, textbooks, and previous reviews across hydrology, geomorphology, ecology, climates, and regions (pp. 1–3, 15). 本文是叙述性、概念性综述，综合了跨水文学、地貌学、生态学、气候区和地区的原始研究、教材及既有综述（第1–3、15页）。
- The synthesis is organized into distinctive hydrological processes, global controls, measurement/modeling challenges, and ten future research directions (pp. 3–15). 内容按特殊水文过程、全球控制因素、观测/建模难点及10项未来研究方向组织（第3–15页）。

### Important methodological choices

- The article does not report a systematic search strategy, inclusion criteria, study count, evidence-quality assessment, or quantitative meta-analysis. Therefore, its conclusions should be read as an expert synthesis rather than an exhaustive systematic review. 论文没有报告系统检索式、纳入标准、研究数量、证据质量评价或定量荟萃分析，因此其结论应视作专家综合，而不是穷尽性的系统综述。
- Numerical examples are drawn from cited studies and illustrate possible magnitudes; they are not globally calibrated parameter values. 文中数值案例来自被引用研究，用于说明可能的量级，不能视为全球率定参数。

# Evidence Worth Remembering | 值得记住的证据

### Evidence 1 — Global importance | 全球重要性
- **Finding:** Approximately half of the world's flowing waterways are categorized as non-perennial. 全球约一半流水水体被归为非永久性。
- **Value / result:** ~50%.
- **Why it matters:** Non-perennial flow is not a marginal exception and should be represented in global models and river concepts. 非永久性并非边缘例外，全球模型和河流理论必须表达它。
- **Page:** 1

### Evidence 2 — No standard classification duration | 没有统一持续时间标准
- **Finding:** No widely used definition specifies how many days or over what reach length surface flow must cease. 没有广泛采用的停流天数或停流河段长度定义。
- **Value / result:** No universal numerical threshold.
- **Why it matters:** Any threshold tested in the current study is an operational hypothesis, not a literature-established truth. 当前研究测试的任何阈值都只是操作性假设，而非已确立结论。
- **Page:** 1

### Evidence 3 — Gauge-network bias | 流量站网络偏差
- **Finding:** Only 3% of USGS gauges and 20% of Global Runoff Data Centre gauges regularly report zero flow. 经常报告零流量的站点仅占USGS站点3%、GRDC站点20%。
- **Value / result:** 3%; 20%.
- **Why it matters:** Gauge-derived calibration or validation sets may systematically underrepresent non-perennial conditions. 基于流量站的率定/验证样本可能系统性低估非永久性条件。
- **Page:** 2

### Evidence 4 — Sediment regime | 泥沙特征
- **Finding:** The cited suspended-sediment concentrations in non-perennial systems range from 35 to 1,700 times those in perennial systems. 文中引用研究显示，非永久性河流悬浮泥沙浓度可为永久性河流的35–1,700倍。
- **Value / result:** 35–1,700×.
- **Why it matters:** Strong sediment mobility can alter channel geometry, destroy sensors, and destabilize discharge rating curves. 强泥沙运动会改变河道几何、破坏传感器并使水位—流量关系失稳。
- **Page:** 2

### Evidence 5 — Evapotranspiration can dominate | 蒸散可占主导
- **Finding:** Plant water use has been reported to account for up to 100% of the perched-aquifer water balance after ephemeral-river floods. 短暂性河流洪水后，植物耗水在案例中可占上层滞水含水层水量平衡的100%。
- **Value / result:** Up to 100% in cited cases.
- **Why it matters:** Apparent channel loss cannot automatically be interpreted as recharge, and ET may drive seasonal cessation. 表观河道损失不能直接解释为补给，蒸散可能驱动季节性停流。
- **Page:** 5

### Evidence 6 — Long records versus sparse events | 长记录与稀少流动事件
- **Finding:** Continuous records of roughly 15–30 years are described as typically preferred for understanding perennial-river flow, whereas non-perennial records may contain only seasonal or rare events. 永久性河流水文认识通常希望有约15–30年连续资料，而非永久性记录可能只有季节性或罕见事件。
- **Value / result:** 15–30 years.
- **Why it matters:** Equal record duration does not imply equal information content when most days have zero flow. 当大多数日期为零流量时，相同记录长度并不意味着相同信息量。
- **Page:** 10

### Evidence 7 — Extreme bed mobility | 极端河床运动
- **Finding:** During a large Salt River flood, sediment 7 m below the streambed was observed to be in motion. Salt River一次大洪水中，河床下7 m的泥沙仍发生运动。
- **Value / result:** 7 m.
- **Why it matters:** Bed-mounted instruments and fixed hydraulic geometry assumptions may fail during the events most important for ephemeral rivers. 对短暂性河流最关键的洪水事件中，河床仪器及固定河道几何假设都可能失效。
- **Page:** 10

# Limitations and Interpretation | 局限与解读

## Authors' cautions | 作者自己的注意事项

- Hydrological knowledge is uneven across river types and ecoregions and remains incomplete (p. 15). 不同河流类型和生态区的认识不均衡，整体知识仍不完整（第15页）。
- The ten future directions are non-exclusive and reflect the authors' collective frame of reference (p. 12). 10项未来方向并非穷尽清单，也受到作者研究视角影响（第12页）。
- Remote sensing often provides temporally sparse snapshots; coarse pixels and canopy cover limit detection of small streams (p. 9). 遥感通常只是稀疏时间快照，粗像元与树冠遮挡限制小溪识别（第9页）。
- Sensors, infiltration measurements, and rating curves can be compromised by scour, deposition, clogging, preferential flow, unsaturated beds, and groundwater changes (pp. 10–11). 冲刷、淤积、堵塞、优先流、非饱和河床和地下水变化会影响传感器、入渗测量与率定曲线（第10–11页）。
- Global river mapping still performs poorly for non-perennial rivers in arid regions and small/headwater catchments (pp. 14–15). 全球河流制图对干旱区及小型/源头非永久性河流表达仍较弱（第14–15页）。

## My interpretation | 我的理解

- This is an expert narrative review, not a systematic review. Absence of a method or threshold from the paper is not proof that none exists anywhere in the literature. 本文是专家叙述综述而非系统综述；论文未提及某种方法或阈值，并不证明整个文献中不存在。
- The review supports the physical plausibility of a size-dependent discharge boundary but does not validate any functional form. `Q_threshold = f(A)` should remain a hypothesis and be compared with richer multivariable models. 本文支持“流量边界可能随河道尺度变化”的物理合理性，但没有验证任何函数形式；`Q_threshold = f(A)`仍应作为假设，并与更丰富的多变量模型比较。
- Cross-sectional area may be endogenous: transmission loss and sediment transport can change channel geometry. Treating `A` as a fixed independent control could obscure feedbacks. 横截面积可能是内生变量，因为传输损失和泥沙输移会改变河道几何；把`A`视作固定独立控制量可能掩盖反馈。
- A JRC-derived perennial label is vulnerable to false permanence where pools remain, and to false dryness for narrow, shaded, turbid, or sub-pixel flow. JRC water occurrence therefore needs ground-based or model-based validation tailored to flow rather than water presence. 若水池长期存在，JRC可能产生“永久性”假阳性；对狭窄、遮荫、浑浊或亚像元水流又可能产生干涸假阳性。因此，JRC water occurrence需要针对“水流”而非“水体存在”的地面或模型验证。
- A global threshold calibrated mainly from existing gauges may inherit their bias toward perennial rivers. Spatial and hydroclimatic stratification is necessary. 主要以现有流量站率定的全球阈值可能继承站网偏向永久性河流的偏差，必须进行空间和水文气候分层。

# Relevance to My Research | 与我的研究的关系

## Directly useful | 可以直接使用

- Distinguish **flowing**, **ponded**, **moist**, and **dry** states when constructing JRC-derived labels; do not map all detectable water to perennial flow (p. 6). 构建JRC标签时区分流动、积水、湿润和干涸状态，不要把所有可检测水体都映射为永久性水流（第6页）。
- Treat `Q/A` or another geometry-normalized hydraulic variable as a candidate, but compare it against groundwater, slope, drainage area, climate, antecedent wetness, streambed properties, and ET controls (pp. 3–8). 可把`Q/A`或其他几何归一化水力变量作为候选，但应与地下水、坡度、汇水面积、气候、前期湿度、河床性质和蒸散控制进行比较（第3–8页）。
- Separate channel water loss into possible groundwater recharge, unsaturated-zone storage, and evapotranspiration instead of assuming all discharge decline is recharge (pp. 4–5). 将河道失水区分为可能的地下水补给、非饱和带储水和蒸散，而不是把所有流量下降都视作补给（第4–5页）。
- Match JRC observations and CaMa-Flood values in time and at reach scale because wetting fronts and active networks move spatially (p. 5). 由于湿润锋和活动河网会移动，应在时间上对齐JRC观测与CaMa-Flood值，并以河段为匹配单元（第5页）。
- Design validation to include ungauged and small/headwater systems rather than relying only on conventional gauge networks (pp. 2, 9–11, 14–15). 验证设计应覆盖无站及小型/源头河流，而不能仅依赖传统流量站网（第2、9–11、14–15页）。

## Important implication | 对我的研究意味着什么

- A single global `Q_threshold` is physically weak because water input competes with size- and setting-dependent losses. Even `Q_threshold = f(A)` may be incomplete unless `A` captures the relevant hydraulic state and the residual effects of groundwater and climate are small. 单一全球`Q_threshold`缺乏充分物理基础，因为水量输入要与随尺度和环境变化的损失竞争；即使使用`Q_threshold = f(A)`，如果`A`不能代表相关水力状态，或地下水与气候的剩余效应很大，该关系仍可能不完整。
- The most informative analysis is not only whether an area-adjusted threshold improves accuracy, but **where and why it fails**—for example in karst, losing rivers, groundwater-fed dry-season reaches, frozen systems, or reaches containing persistent pools. 最有价值的分析不仅是面积修正阈值是否提高准确率，还应识别其在何处、为何失败，例如喀斯特河流、失水河流、旱季地下水补给河段、冻结系统或含永久水池的河段。
- Classification uncertainty should be stratified by river size, climate, groundwater regime, and remote-sensing detectability. 分类不确定性应按河流尺度、气候、地下水状态和遥感可探测性分层。

## Not directly transferable | 不能直接照搬

- The paper provides no CaMa-Flood parameterization, cross-sectional-area formula, discharge threshold, fitted permanence classifier, or accuracy statistic. 本文没有提供CaMa-Flood参数化、横截面积公式、流量阈值、拟合的持续性分类器或准确率指标。
- It does not evaluate JRC Global Surface Water, HydroRIVERS, or a global river-network matching algorithm. 本文未评估JRC Global Surface Water、HydroRIVERS或全球河网匹配算法。
- Numerical examples from individual catchments cannot be transferred as universal parameters. 单个流域案例中的数值不能作为全球通用参数。

# What I Can Reuse | 可以借鉴什么

- **Concept / definition:** Flow regime as magnitude + frequency + duration + timing + rate of change; non-perennial flow as lack of continuous annual surface flow (pp. 1, 3). 流态五维定义，以及非永久性水流的年度非连续性概念（第1、3页）。
- **Physical mechanism:** Reach water balance between runoff/interflow/baseflow/upstream inflow and infiltration/storage/ET losses (pp. 3–5). 河段输入与入渗、储存和蒸散损失之间的水量平衡机制（第3–5页）。
- **Method:** Combine manual wet/dry surveys, automated sensors, gauges, remote sensing, and models rather than treating any one source as ground truth (pp. 9–12). 联合人工干湿调查、自动传感器、流量站、遥感和模型，而不是把单一数据源当作绝对真值（第9–12页）。
- **Validation idea:** Validate JRC-derived classes against sensors or repeated field observations and stratify failures by wetting mode, groundwater connectivity, stream size, and sediment mobility. 用传感器或重复地面观测验证JRC类别，并按湿润模式、地下水联系、河流尺度和泥沙活动性分析失败类型。
- **Figure / visualization idea:** Use Figure 2's three network-wetting modes as a spatial error taxonomy; compare discharge–area boundaries across hydroclimatic and groundwater strata. 用图2的三种河网湿润模式建立空间误差分类，并跨水文气候和地下水分层比较流量—面积边界。
- **Citation / argument:** Cite the paper to justify why no universal duration or discharge criterion should be assumed and why surface-water occurrence is not equivalent to connected flow (pp. 1, 6). 用本文论证不能预设通用持续时间/流量标准，以及地表水存在不等于连通水流（第1、6页）。
